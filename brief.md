# Smart Contact Manager (SCM) — Project Brief

## What

Smart Contact Manager (SCM) is a web-based contact management
system that allows users to securely store, organize, search,
update, and manage their contacts in one place.

The system provides user authentication and account management,
including registration, email verification, and login through
Google and GitHub. Users can create contacts, add contact
information and pictures, view contact details, edit or delete
contacts, search contacts, and mark important contacts as
favorites.

The system also supports communication and data management
features. Users can send emails with attachments, upload and
delete contact pictures, paginate through contacts, and export
contact information to Excel. Users can also manage their
profiles, change themes, and provide feedback.

## Who

The main users of Smart Contact Manager are individuals who want
to maintain and manage their personal or professional contacts
digitally.

The system is intended for authenticated users who need a
centralized application for storing contact information,
organizing contacts, searching for contacts, and communicating
with them.

managing contact information and related communication tasks.
The system combines authentication, contact management, search,
file handling, email communication, data export, and user
